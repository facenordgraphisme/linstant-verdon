Unusual

Sunset Cliffside Portaledge Evening

Live the unforgettable experience of an aperitif suspended in the middle of Verdon's giant cliffs.

Description

Experience the unique evening of your dreams suspended in a portaledge.

The Verdon Gorge, a world-renowned climbing destination. A portaledge is a temporary platform suspended from the cliff face, historically used by climbers who slept on the rock face.

You'll enjoy a breathtaking view overlooking the Verdon River with its turquoise waters. Near the vertiginous cliffs, you can watch vultures soaring overhead, climbers, and the stunning views of the Verdon Gorge and Blanc Martel.

Savor a unique dinner or a light dinner with a front-row seat to a magnificent landscape while enjoying the sunset and pleasant temperatures!

This adventure is accessible to all levels, as access is easy from the top of the main climbing routes. You'll be equipped with harnesses, helmets, and adjustable lanyards to prepare you for a vertical experience and feel the sensations of a true mountaineer!

Meals are not included in the package; however, the village restaurants offer takeaway meals, pizzas, and drinks.

The meeting point is at the start of the Route des Crêtes (Crest Road).

You will be secured throughout your activity with professional equipment.

A photographer may be on site; for more information, please see her online gallery.
