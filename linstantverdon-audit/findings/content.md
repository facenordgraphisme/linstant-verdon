## Thin Content

### Blog posts: critically thin (~320–350 words each)
**Observed:** Both blog articles are approximately 320–350 words long:
- "Les 3 activités à faire dans le Verdon cet été" — ~350 words
- "Les 3 activités à faire dans le Verdon ce printemps" — ~320 words

**Benchmark:** Top-ranking French outdoor tourism articles average 1,200–2,000 words. Competitors (voyagetips.com, checkyeti.com) publish comprehensive guides that displace thin posts.

**Impact:** Thin content rarely earns featured snippets, image carousels, or "People also ask" appearances. These posts will not rank.

**Fix:** Expand each blog post to 1,000–1,500 words minimum:
- Add practical details for each activity (duration, difficulty, what to bring, price range, age minimum)
- Include FAQ section at the end (5–8 questions)
- Add context about the location / Gorges du Verdon
- Include author voice and personal experience (E-E-A-T)

### FAQ page: critically thin (3 questions)
**Observed:** Only 3 FAQ items found:
1. What equipment to bring
2. Must know how to swim?
3. Cancellation conditions (deferred to CGV)

**Industry standard:** FAQ pages for activity providers typically cover 15–25 questions.

**Missing FAQ topics:**
- Minimum/maximum age by activity
- Meeting point / how to get there
- Group size limits
- Weather cancellation policy
- Difficulty levels by canyon/route
- What is provided (wetsuit, helmet, harness)
- Booking in advance recommendations
- Activities for children / families
- Gift vouchers / corporate bookings
